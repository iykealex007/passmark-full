Are these real past questions?
+
Yes. Every question in Passmark comes from official past papers. For school exams like WAEC and JAMB, questions go back as far as 1983. For professional exams like ICAN, we use the official Pathfinder questions published by the exam body.
Can I use Passmark on my phone?
+
Absolutely. Passmark is fully responsive and works great on Android and iPhone. You don't need to download any app — just open it in your browser and start practising.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
We accept all major Nigerian debit and credit cards, as well as bank transfers, via Paystack. All transactions are secure and encrypted. You pay in Naira.
Is there a free trial?
+
Yes! You can create a free account and practise with a limited set of questions for each exam before subscribing. No credit card required to sign up.
Can I switch my plan later?
+
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. If you upgrade mid-cycle, we'll credit the unused portion of your current plan.
How often are questions updated?
+
We add new questions after every major exam sitting. For JAMB and WAEC, new questions are usually added within 2–4 weeks of results being released.
